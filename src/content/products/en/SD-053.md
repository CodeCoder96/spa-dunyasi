## How a vichy shower works

The guest lies on the bed of the unit. The 6 heads fitted to the overhead bar spread water like rain along the body, from the shoulders down to the feet. The session can be water only, or the therapist can give a massage while the rain shower runs. That flexibility lets one unit serve several different treatment menus in the same room.

A vichy shower is typically planned alongside treatments such as:

- Rinsing after a body scrub
- Rinsing off mud and other body masks
- Massage rituals under the rain shower
- Thalasso and body care packages

## Mosaic and marble look

As its name suggests, this model points to mosaic and marble surfaces. Since these materials are common in hammams and wet areas, the unit speaks the same language as the wall and floor finishes of those spaces. The wooden parts are made from water- and moisture-resistant timber and can be ordered in different colours.

If you prefer a unit with a fully timber look, see the [Wood vichy shower](urun:SD-055).

## Planning the room

The 80 cm height lets a therapist working on their feet reach both sides of the bed comfortably. The 215 cm length and 95 cm width are the figures to start from when sizing the room, and you should allow clear space for the therapist to move around the unit. Water supply, drainage and floor falls all need to be settled at the design stage.

Adding a shower and a small rest corner next to the room lets a guest dry off and get ready without leaving the wet zone. If possible, also plan a trolley or shelf at the head end for towels and products.
