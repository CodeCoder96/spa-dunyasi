## The name and the numbers

Choosing a product by its name is easy but can be misleading. The High may sound like a taller bed, yet our catalogue lists it with an 80 × 200 cm surface and a 68-85 cm height, the same values as the [Dynamic](urun:SD-009) and the Classic. When comparing, focus on the motor version and what your room needs rather than the model name.

## Face-down work

In daily use, the defining feature of the High is its face hole. For work on the back, lower back and shoulders, the guest lies face down; the face hole lets them rest without twisting their head, keeping the neck in a straight line. Over a long session, that makes a noticeable difference to guest comfort.

The upholstery is Vinleks over 30 DNS foam, the cushion is 10 cm thick and the body is a water- and moisture-resistant timber.

## Physiotherapy practices and sports massage

In physiotherapy practices and studios offering sports massage, a bed carries clients of many different builds, and a 350 kg load capacity gives reassurance in that setting. When choosing between the fixed, single-motor and two-motor versions, think about the height differences between your therapists and how often clients change position during a session.

Because it is also suitable for skincare and body treatments, the High can serve beauty rooms if your practice offers those services too.

## Preparing for a quote

When asking for a price, tell us how many rooms you have, how your therapists work and how much of your treatment time is spent face down. That makes the motor choice clear. If you pick the timber and upholstery colours at the same time, we can settle the quote in one go.
