## Matching your salon's palette

A pedicure chair is one of the bulkiest, most visible pieces in a salon, often the first thing people notice from the door. Its colour should not be left to chance. Because the wood and upholstery colours on the Wood can be set separately, you can tie the chair in with your flooring, wall panels or reception desk.

A pale wood with darker upholstery gives a balanced contrast in a clean, modern salon. A warm-toned wood with earthy upholstery suits a cosier boutique feel. A few photos of your salon are all we need to help you decide.

## Sized to your space

Every salon has its own walls, columns and door positions. Since the Wood's dimensions can be adapted on request, it can be planned into an alcove, in front of a partition wall or into a narrow room. If you want a size change, give us the width and depth of the spot and any obstacles around it. A simple hand-drawn sketch and a few photos help us judge quickly whether the change is feasible. If you are ordering several chairs, say at the outset whether they should all share one size or differ, which keeps the quoting process simple.

## The Wood in daily use

The chair has a spa basin for the guest's feet, so preparation and treatment are completed in one place. While one person does the pedicure, another can do a manicure. The chair height is also set to suit the person working, which helps in salons where several therapists use the same chair through the day.
