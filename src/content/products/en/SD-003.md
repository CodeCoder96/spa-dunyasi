## Keeping supplies under the bed

In small treatment rooms, the usual headache is finding somewhere for towels and oil bottles. A separate shelf or trolley takes up floor space and has the therapist walking around the room mid-session. On the Base W, those supplies sit directly beneath the bed, so the therapist can reach down for what they need without leaving the guest.

A tidy compartment also tells the guest that the room is well organised. With peshtemals and towels folded in rows and the pillow kept to one side, setting up for the next guest takes only a few moments.

## An MDF body for humid rooms

The body is made from water- and moisture-resistant MDF. That matters in rooms near a hammam or steam room, and on surfaces that are wiped down often after oil treatments. Because the colours of the wood and upholstery are chosen to order, you can match the bed to your floor and wall finishes.

## What it is used for

Base W works for massage, body care and spa treatments as well as skincare. If you want guests semi-reclined during facials, add the manual backrest lift. Heating, seven-colour LED therapy lighting, a bolster and a protective cover are among the other extras available.

## When you ask for a quote

Tell us the size of your room, the treatments you plan to offer and your preferred wood and upholstery shades. If you are fitting out several rooms, you can list different extras for each one, for example heating in one and a backrest lift in another. With that information we can suggest a configuration without adding anything you do not need.
