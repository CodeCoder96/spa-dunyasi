## Rest furniture ready for the open air

Most of the loungers in our catalogue are designed for indoor spa areas and the edges of wet zones. The rattan sun lounger stands out as the one made for outdoor use. Its UV-filtered surface coating addresses a key concern for furniture left in the sun for long periods, and because the build withstands heat and cold, it is easier to keep outside from one season to the next.

The load-bearing parts use water- and moisture-resistant timber, an important detail for a piece people sit on in wet swimwear beside the pool.

## Where it works

The rattan sun lounger has a look that adapts to many settings:

- Hotel and villa pool decks
- Terraces, balconies and garden seating areas
- Outdoor sections of cafés and restaurants
- Spa rest lounges and massage rooms

Indoors, the rattan texture feels lighter and more natural than leather-upholstered loungers. In a spa lounge that opens onto a garden, you can furnish the inside and the outside with the same piece. In homes and villas, the same lounger can serve both the living room and the pool deck, which makes it easy to build a consistent outdoor scheme around one model. It is also a practical choice for businesses that rearrange their outdoor space at the start of each season.
