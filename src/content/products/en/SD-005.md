## One bed, two room layouts

A newly opened beauty salon rarely has a separate room for every treatment. The room used for facials in the morning may host a back massage in the afternoon. Base X is built around that flexibility. With the backrest flat, it works like a standard massage bed; with the manual backrest lift added, the guest can be brought up into a semi-reclined position and the beautician can reach the face comfortably.

It is also a practical route for businesses that want to test demand before investing in a dedicated [beauty treatment chair](urun:SD-032).

## The practical side of the body

The body is water- and moisture-resistant MDF. Treatments involving oils, toners or masks mean surfaces are wiped down often, and this choice of material suits that routine. The lower part of the bed holds a massage pillow, oil, towels and peshtemals, so supplies for both massage and skincare stay close by.

The cushion is 10 cm thick and the surface measures 80 × 200 cm.

## Completing the room

Heating is worth considering for facials, where the guest lies still for a long time. The seven-colour LED therapy light is for venues that like to change the mood of the room. A bolster, protective cover, drawer and segmented cushion are also on the list. Once you have chosen the wood and upholstery colours, you can send everything to us in one message.

## Questions to ask before choosing

How much of the room's schedule goes to facials and how much to body massage? Do guests lie face up or face down for most sessions? Is the room cool, or comfortable without heating? Share your answers and we can work out together whether the backrest lift and heating are really needed.
