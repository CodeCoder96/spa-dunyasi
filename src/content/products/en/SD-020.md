## Built around a busy salon day

In a massage salon, a bed hosts one guest after another all day long. The first expectation at that pace is that the guest feels at ease lying face down and that the cushion gives the same support in every session. The Trend's specification was chosen with that in mind. The face hole means the guest does not have to turn their head to the side during face-down massage, and the 30 DNS foam with a 10 cm cushion gives an even surface to lie on.

The frame timber is a water- and moisture-resistant grade. In rooms where oil and lotion are used heavily, this detail deserves a place in your buying decision.

## Configuring step by step

When preparing your Trend order, you can work through it in this order:

1. **Motor build:** a fixed frame where no adjustment is needed, or one or two motors if the guest is repositioned often.
2. **Extras:** choose from heating, a bolster, a protective cover, seven-colour LED therapy lighting, a drawer and a segmented cushion.
3. **Colour:** pick timber and upholstery tones that suit your cabin.
4. **Quantity:** confirm how many rooms you are equipping.

Sending all four in a single message helps us prepare your quote more quickly. If you are unsure about a step, for instance whether your cabin needs heating, simply describe the treatments you offer and we will weigh up the options with you.

## Who chooses the Trend?

The Trend is a balanced option for businesses that do a lot of face-down massage without needing much repositioning. Massage salons, the body massage rooms of hotel spas and the treatment areas of physiotherapy centres are typical users. For skincare cabins where the guest changes position frequently, the three-motor [Nice](urun:SD-014) is also worth considering.
