## A different treatment for your spa menu

Warm sand and salt treatments appeal to businesses that want to offer an experience distinct from classic massage. The guest lies on heated quartz sand or salt, which spreads beneath the body and forms a soft bed that settles around them. The Trend Salt & Sand Bed is made so that you can offer this experience in a cabin on a regular basis.

Because the treatment is distinctive, hotel spas and wellness centres can list it as a separate item in their packages. In your guest-facing descriptions, we suggest describing the experience the bed offers rather than promising any health outcome.

## Working height for the therapist

During sand or salt treatments the therapist usually works standing beside the bed. Adjustable height means therapists of different heights can find a working level that suits them. The catalogue height range is 68-85 cm.

Our catalogue also describes the bed as suitable for skincare and body treatments. You can therefore plan the care steps that come before or after a sand or salt session in the same cabin, and complete the programme without moving the guest to another room.

## Sized to your space

The standard surface is 80 × 200 cm, but the dimensions can be changed on request. If you want a wider surface, or need the bed to fit an existing cabin, send us the room measurements and the width of the door. Telling us where the power supply will come into the cabin also helps us plan the heating configuration properly.

We are happy to talk through the practical details with you at the quote stage, such as how the sand or salt is placed in the bed, how it is levelled between treatments and how it is replaced from time to time.
