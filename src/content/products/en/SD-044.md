## Who needs a cold towel service?

A chilled towel can be as much a part of the guest experience as a warm one. Handing a cool towel to a guest by the pool, in the gym, on the way out of a sauna or hammam, or at reception on a hot day is a common touch in hotels and resorts. The Towel Cooler is built for exactly that, letting you prepare towels in advance and keep them cool all day.

What sets it apart is compressor cooling that holds its value in warm surroundings. The catalogue gives a cooling range of +2 /+8 degrees at an ambient temperature of 43 degrees. That makes it an option not only for air-conditioned interiors but also for spaces close to outdoor service points in hot destinations.

## Quiet in guest areas

The cabinet usually stands where guests are. A noise level of 38/44 decibels is a figure to keep in mind for calm settings such as a relaxation lounge or a spa reception. The energy class is listed as A++ and A+++.

## Details that help day to day

- The sensor-activated LED light comes on when the door opens, so towels are easy to see in dimly lit rooms.
- The automatic timer and automatic defrost cut down on routine attention.
- Interior and door shelves are adjustable, so you can set different heights for rolled and folded towels.
- The reversible door lets you position the cabinet to suit the layout of the room.
