## Soft or Lux Soft?

In our catalogue, the Soft and the [Lux Soft](urun:SD-013) have identical technical data: the same size, the same load capacity, the same motor layout and the same list of extras. For that reason we do not claim any difference in how they perform. The decision comes down to how the body design will look in your cabin. Look at the product photos side by side, and if you are still unsure, send us a photo of your room so we can talk through which one would sit better there.

If you do not want armrests, the [Nice](urun:SD-014), which also has a curved body and three motors, is an open-sided alternative.

## Why the angles matter

Lifting the backrest to 35° brings the guest into a semi-reclined position. That is useful for facials, mask applications and any part of a treatment where the guest likes to chat. Being able to raise the footrest to 25° gives you an extra setting for treatments where you do not want the legs to lie flat.

Because the height is also motorised, the therapist can set the bed to suit working on their feet or sitting on a stool. The catalogue height range is 68-85 cm.

## The balance armrests bring

During long face-up treatments, the armrests give the guest's arms a comfortable place beside the bed. For massage, the spring mechanism lets you lower them so they never stop the therapist from reaching the edge. In cabins that handle both skincare and body work, this two-way use makes the day easier.

## Which extras to choose

Pick extras to suit how your cabin runs. Heating adds comfort during treatments where the guest stays still for a long time. A protective cover and a segmented cushion help you turn the bed around quickly between different treatments. A drawer keeps the towels and products you reach for most within easy reach. Venues that like to set the mood with light can opt for the seven-colour LED therapy lighting.
