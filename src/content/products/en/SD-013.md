## Who it suits

Lux Soft is designed for rooms where one bed has to cover several kinds of work in a day: a massage room, a facial cabin or a hotel spa. Because it suits both skincare and body treatments, the same model can serve a beauty salon offering facials and a day spa offering full-body massage.

The first thing that sets it apart is the base. The curved timber body looks softer in a room than a box-shaped cabinet, and the timber is a water- and moisture-resistant grade, which matters in cabins where oils are used and the air stays humid.

## Day-to-day details

With three motors, the therapist can position the bed without lifting or cranking anything. Height moves within the 68-85 cm range to match the therapist and the treatment. Raising the backrest brings the guest into a semi-seated position, and the separately lifting footrest makes longer facials or combined hand treatments more comfortable.

The armrests drop out of the way when they are not needed, so they do not block techniques that require standing close to the bed. The cushion is 10 cm thick.

## Configuring it

You can tailor the Lux Soft to how your business works. Heating is popular in cooler months. Bolsters (cylinder and half-moon), a protective cover and a segmented cushion make daily use easier. A drawer keeps towels and oils at hand, and the seven-colour LED therapy lighting is chosen by venues that like to change the mood of the room.

If you are not sure which extras you need, tell us the size of your room and the treatments you offer, and we will help you decide.
