## Three functions at one workstation

During a hot stone session the therapist needs three things close at hand: heated stones, warm massage oil and, for some treatments, massage balls. A separate unit for each crowds the worktop and needs more than one socket. With the three heating functions listed in its catalogue entry, this heater brings all of them to one spot.

In practice you work from a single unit on the side table or trolley next to the massage bed. You take a stone, use it, put it back, and top up the oil from the same heater.

## Compared with Pan

This model and the [Pan Stone Heater](urun:SD-050) share the same body size (a 40 × 25 cm base and 12 cm height) and the same 220V/180W rating. Both heat within 55-80°. The only difference is that oil heating also appears in this model's feature list.

If your menu includes treatments with warmed oil, or you would rather your therapists did not warm oil in a separate container, this model will be the more practical choice.

## Setting up the room

- Place the unit on the side where the therapist works most, not at the head or foot of the bed.
- Plan a small tray area for picking up and setting down oil and stones.
- Set the heating level according to your stones and the oil maker's guidance.
