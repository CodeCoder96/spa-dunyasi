## Designing with glass mosaic

What sets this model apart from our other loungers is its surface. The lounger is clad in glass mosaic, and glass mosaic offers a broad palette. You can plan the lounger in a single flat colour, as a blend of several shades or in a pattern that echoes your brand colours.

A few questions make the colour plan easier:

- Should the lounger merge with the wall and floor finishes, or stand out as a feature?
- If there are several loungers, will they all share one colour?
- Is the lighting warm or cool? Glass reflects light, so the chosen shade will look different under each.

## Continuity with pool and hammam

Pools, hammams and steam rooms are often finished in mosaic. A rest lounger in the same material gives a seamless look right next to these areas. You might repeat the tones of the pool lining in the poolside loungers, or ask for cladding that echoes the pattern around the hammam basins at the hammam exit.

The 45 cm height lets guests sit down and lie back easily, and the 200 cm length gives enough room to stretch out fully.

## Heating and trolley

The heating option keeps the glass mosaic surface warm in cooler lounges. A trolley provides a surface beside the lounger for towels and personal items. As dimensions can be adapted on request, the lounger can also be sized to fit an alcove or a built-in bench along a wall.
