## Why nail extensions call for a proper table

Gel, acrylic and extension work takes far longer than a standard manicure and uses far more kit. Tips, forms, brushes, powders, liquids, files, a curing lamp and a desk lamp all need to be within reach at once. On a narrow surface they pile on top of each other and the technician spends the session shuffling things aside.

Glass gives this kind of work enough room. Along the 130 cm surface you can line up supplies in the order you use them and keep the area where the guest rests their hands clear and clean. Being offered as suitable for nail extensions makes it a sensible starting point for salons where this is a main source of income.

## The model name and the look

Glass is listed with the same dimensions as the Light and Wood manicure tables in our catalogue. When choosing among the three, we suggest looking at appearance rather than hunting for a technical difference: view the product photos side by side and decide which fits the furniture style of your salon. Being able to choose the wood colour also makes it easier to match the table to its surroundings.

## Working around the table

With the technician and guest facing each other, each side needs space for a seat. As the table stands 82 cm high, pairing it with a seat whose height can be adjusted lets you set posture relative to the table. Our [manicure chair](urun:SD-040) is worth a look for this.

If space is tight or you need a non-standard size, the dimensions can be adapted on request. Tell us the length of the wall where the table will go, where the sockets are and any nearby windows or light fittings, and we can think through a layout that also keeps lamp and dryer cables tidy.
