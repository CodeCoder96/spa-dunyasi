## Who uses a massage chair?

A massage chair is the tool of therapists who take their service to the guest, and of businesses that offer short sessions. Typical settings include:

- **Mobile therapists:** a working point you can carry with you to homes and hotel rooms.
- **Corporate events:** short massage breaks that companies arrange for staff at the office.
- **Exhibitions and promotions:** a compact setup on a stand where guests can sit down without changing.
- **Spas and salons:** an extra option next to the bed for quick sessions in a waiting area or outside the cabin.

## The practical side of seated massage

Seated sessions do not require the guest to undress or the therapist to use oil, so they work well for short treatments done fully clothed. That is why the service can also be offered in places where a cabin cannot be set up, such as an office floor, a shopping centre or an airport lounge. When the session ends, the guest simply carries on with their day.

The guest rests their chest and face against the front support, and the therapist works comfortably from behind or from the side. At a busy event, this arrangement makes it easier to welcome a steady stream of guests one after another.
