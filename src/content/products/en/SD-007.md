## Fixed or motorised?

When buying a massage bed, most owners start by asking whether a motor is really necessary. Classic answers that question in three ways.

### Fixed

A straightforward choice for small salons and home treatment rooms where one therapist delivers the same kind of massage. It needs no power connection, so you can place it anywhere in the room.

### Single motor

Suited to businesses where several therapists share the same bed during the day and want to reposition it without doing it by hand.

### Two motors

If the room hosts both massage and other treatments, and the guest's position changes several times in a session, the two-motor build gives you more flexibility.

We will confirm exactly which sections are motor-driven when we prepare your quote.

## Face hole and upholstery

The head end of the Classic has a face hole, so during back and shoulder massage the guest does not have to turn their head to one side. The upholstery combines Vinleks with 30 DNS foam, and the cushion is 10 cm thick. The body is made from a water- and moisture-resistant timber, which counts in rooms where oils and lotions are used.

## Which salons it suits

Classic is designed for massage salons, beauty centres and hotel spas offering both skincare and body treatments. With colour options for the timber and upholstery, you can line up beds that match your existing interior. Add heating, a bolster or a protective cover to fit the bed to your own way of working.

Before ordering, it helps to note how many therapists will share the bed and how often guests change position in a typical session. With that, choosing between the three versions is usually simple.
