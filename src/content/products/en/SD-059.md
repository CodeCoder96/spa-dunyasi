## Keeping a cabin organised

During a facial or a massage, everything the therapist needs (creams, oils, towels, cotton pads and small tools) has to sit somewhere. Left on a windowsill or at the foot of the bed, these items make the room look cluttered and keep the therapist moving back and forth. A trolley gathers them in one place.

Room sizes, treatment menus and interior styles vary from venue to venue, so one design rarely suits everyone. That is why the range runs to eleven versions.

## Getting to know the versions

**Beautician trolley:** intended for skincare cabins, where a large number of small products need to stay in order through a facial.

**Thai trolley:** reserved for rooms offering Thai massage, for venues that want a piece in keeping with the particular layout of those rooms.

**Models 1 to 9:** nine numbered designs with different forms and looks. Put the product photos side by side and pick the one that matches the furniture already in your cabins.

## Choosing the right one

When you compare models, think about the free floor space in the room, where the bed or chair stands and how much stock you like to keep within reach during the day. You can choose different models for different rooms within the same venue.

If you are opening a new branch or refreshing existing cabins, using the same model throughout gives the business a consistent look, and staff find their supplies in the same place in every room. Where rooms differ a lot, you can choose models by treatment and tie them together with a shared colour scheme.
