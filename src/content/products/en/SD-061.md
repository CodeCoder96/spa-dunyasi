## The wet swimsuit problem

Hotels with indoor pools, spa centres and gyms all share a small but frequent nuisance: the wet swimsuit. Packed straight into a bag, it soaks everything else; left in the changing room, it leaves puddles on the floor and in the lockers. A swimsuit dryer removes most of the water before the guest even reaches the changing area, which deals with the issue where it starts.

According to the catalogue, a cycle lasts 10 seconds and takes out 95% of the moisture. Such a short cycle helps avoid queues at the pool exit, so even at busy times guests can use the machine one after another. It handles bikinis and shorts as well, so you do not need a separate solution for different kinds of swimwear.

For guests, it is a small detail that makes the end of their visit tidier. For the venue, less water carried into the changing rooms means drier floors and an easier job for the cleaning team.

## Build and installation

The body is stainless steel and the fittings are PE plastic, a sensible pairing for a machine that is in constant contact with water. It weighs 25 kg and runs on a 220VAC-50Hz mains supply. Its dimensions are listed in the technical table.

A good location is usually near the showers or the pool exit, on the route to the changing room, so guests can dry their swimwear and go straight to their locker. We recommend positioning the socket according to local electrical regulations so that water from the wet floor cannot reach the connection.
