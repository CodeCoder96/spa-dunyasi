## Open sides, uninterrupted working space

Some massage techniques need the therapist to stand right at the edge of the bed or to move the guest's arm out beyond it. On a model with armrests, folding them away becomes part of every session. Because the Nice's catalogue description has no armrests, both long edges are clear from end to end. If most of your sessions are body massage or back work, this simple layout can suit you well.

If you would rather have armrests, the [Soft](urun:SD-017) and [Lux Soft](urun:SD-013) share the same approach to the body and are a better match.

## Three motors for a flexible day

The Nice has three separate motors. From the hand control you can raise and lower the bed, lift the backrest and position the footrest on its own. That means the same cabin can be set up for a facial in the morning and a flat body massage in the afternoon. When shifts change, the next therapist simply resets the working height to suit themselves.

The curved body gives the room a softer outline than a box-shaped bed. The timber parts are made from a water- and moisture-resistant material, so the bed is at home in rooms where oil-based treatments are frequent.

## Before you order

- Decide whether you want heating; it adds guest comfort in cooler rooms.
- Think about whether extras such as the drawer and LED lighting fit the layout of your cabin.
- Plan how a bolster and a segmented cushion would be used in your face-down and face-up treatments.
- Consider whether a protective cover would help, given how many different treatments the bed will host each day.

Just send us the size of your room and the treatments you offer. If you also tell us where the power supply will reach the bed, we can work out together how the three-motor setup fits into your space.
