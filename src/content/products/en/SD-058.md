## A small piece that tidies the room

A good part of a spa visit happens outside the treatment rooms, in the relaxation area. After the hammam, the sauna or a massage, guests lie down for a while and want to sip some tea, check their phone or read. Without a surface beside the bed, those things end up on the floor or scattered over the cushion.

A side table deals with that small but easily overlooked need. Belongings stay within arm's reach and the room looks more orderly. Staff benefit too, because empty glasses and used towels are easier to collect from one spot.

## Planning the layout

Where the tables go depends on how the loungers are arranged. In a straight row, placing one table between each pair of beds saves floor space and keeps the line neat. In looser layouts, such as a poolside terrace or a large lounge, each bed can have its own table.

Leave enough room to walk past so guests do not bump into the table as they get up. It also helps if whatever is on the table can still be reached when the backrest of the lounger is raised.

Since no dimensions are published, the simplest approach is to send us the gap between your loungers along with a floor plan. You can also judge from the product photo whether the shape fits the look of your space. If the table will stand close to a wet area, mention that in your enquiry.
