## A colour plan across two materials

With the Moon you make two colour decisions: one for the timber base and one for the leather upholstery. That flexibility makes it easier to fit the lounger into the palette of your space. Match the timber to the flooring, doors or cabinet fronts, and the leather to curtains, towels and wall colours.

A few approaches that work well:

- **Tone on tone:** timber and leather in closely related shades give a calm, unified look, ideal for quiet rest rooms.
- **Contrast:** light leather on dark timber, or the reverse, brings out the shape of the lounger and makes a striking row in a large, bright pool hall.
- **Zone colours:** order the same model in two leather colours to separate women's and men's areas, or to mark out a VIP corner.

## The backrest in daily use

The backrest is raised by hand and reaches 35°. Guests can switch between lying flat and sitting half upright on their own. With no power connection needed, you can position loungers without worrying about where the sockets are.

Sitting low at 45 cm, the Moon is upholstered with a 10 cm cushion over 32 DNS foam. The base is water- and moisture-resistant timber, so it also works in the damp zones just outside a steam room or hammam. A trolley can be added beside each lounger, and a protective leather cover is available for heavily used rooms.
