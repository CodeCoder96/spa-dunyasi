## Three sizes for different ways of working

A unit next to a manicure table in a small salon does not need to be the same size as one in the preparation room of a hotel spa. The three volume options of the Steriliser Cabinet are there to cover that difference.

The Mini model is worth considering for stations with very little worktop space. The 5-litre cabinet fits a single workstation, while the 16-litre cabinet is better suited to preparation rooms that get items ready for several treatments at once. When choosing, think not only about today's workload but also about whether several practitioners will share the same unit.

## What is inside

There are two main components: the heating element and the UV filter. The technical table gives a cabinet temperature of 70 °C – 80°C. The unit weighs 6 kg and connects to a 220-volt supply. The outer dimensions are shown in the table on this page, so compare them with your worktop before deciding where it goes.

The cabinet is not offered as a stand-alone answer for procedures that require formal health or disinfection documentation. Any procedures your sector's rules ask for still need to be followed within your own working routine.

## Where it is used

Hair and beauty salons, manicure and pedicure studios, massage studios and hotel spas tend to keep this kind of cabinet close to the workstation.
