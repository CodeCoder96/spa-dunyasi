## Two practitioners, one guest

In busy beauty salons, hand and foot care is a service that often squeezes the diary. Done one after the other, the two treatments keep a guest in the salon for a long time and the chair serves fewer people each day. Spa Life addresses this with a seating layout where both can run together.

The pedicurist works in front of the foot spa basin while the manicurist sits beside the chair. For the guest, that means one appointment and less waiting. If you set this up as its own package in your booking system and block out both staff members together, scheduling becomes simpler and reception can tell guests exactly when two specialists will be free. For the business, the arrangement is a practical way to get more out of the same chair each day.

## Which businesses is it for?

- Beauty centres that sell manicure and pedicure as a package
- Hotel spas wanting to offer resident guests hand and foot care in a short visit
- Salons with time-pressured services such as bridal preparation or special-occasion packages

In these settings, leave enough room around the chair for two people to settle in comfortably. For the manicure side, you can plan it together with a table such as our [Light manicure table](urun:SD-039).

## Height adjustment for the practitioner

The Spa Life's height can be set to a level where the therapist can work with a comfortable posture. Through a day of back-to-back bookings, this saves staff from constantly stooping.
