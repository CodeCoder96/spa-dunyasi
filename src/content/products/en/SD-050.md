## What Pan is for

A therapist offering hot stone work needs one thing above all: stones kept at the chosen temperature for the whole session. Pan is a heater built for that job. You place the stones in the tank and set the heating level; during the treatment you take out the stone you need and return used ones so they can warm up again.

It is not limited to stones. Pan also warms massage balls, so the same unit supports both stone-based and ball-based treatments.

## Size and placement

Pan measures 12 cm high, 25 cm wide and 40 cm long. That footprint means it fits on a side table next to the massage bed or on a trolley. Heating runs at 220V/180W, so you will need a socket within reach in the treatment room.

A few points make placement easier:

- Put the unit where the therapist can reach it but where guests will not brush against it when getting onto the bed.
- Keep the cable clear of walkways.
- Leave a small towel or tray area beside it for picking up and setting down stones.

## Before each session

Set the heating level within the 55-80° range to suit your stones and your treatment routine. Checking each stone's temperature with your own hand before it touches the guest is a sensible habit for safe practice.
