## Why stones of different sizes?

A hot stone session is never run with a single type of stone. Therapists tend to use large, flat stones on broad areas and smaller ones around the hands, feet and face. This set brings together stones of varied shape and size, giving you the range one session calls for. Whether a therapist is new to the technique or experienced, they can pick the pieces that suit their own way of working.

The set weighs 8 kg net. That figure is useful when you plan how the stones will sit in the heater and how many sets you need.

## Plan it with a heater

Stones are not a stand-alone piece of kit; in practice they are always paired with a heater. Once you have the set, you will need a unit that keeps the stones warm through the session. Our catalogue offers two options for that:

- the Pan model, if you will only warm stones and massage balls;
- the oil and stone heater, if you also want to warm massage oil in the same place.

Asking for the heater and the set in the same quote makes it easier to confirm that they suit each other from the start.

## Care and storage

After each session, wipe the remaining oil off the stones, dry them and keep them in a box or on a tray until the next use; this keeps the set tidy over time. Setting aside any stone that is cracked or chipped is also a good habit.
