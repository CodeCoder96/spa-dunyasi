## What wood brings to a salon

Nail salons often choose white, glossy surfaces for their furniture. Wood appeals to businesses after a warmer, more natural feel. For boutique salons, hotel spa treatment rooms or studios built around natural materials, the wooden part of the table becomes something that rounds off the atmosphere of the room.

Since the wood colour is yours to choose, you can match the table to your parquet, shelving, or door and window frames. A pale wood keeps a salon feeling airy, while a darker tone gives a more grounded impression. We recommend checking with us before ordering which tones are currently available.

If you also offer pedicures, staying within one colour family for a wood-detailed pedicure chair creates continuity between the two stations. If you are thinking of ordering this table alongside the [Wood pedicure chair](urun:SD-037), whose wood and upholstery colours can both be chosen, simply name the tone you want for both in the same message and we will go through options for a coordinated set.

## A size to suit your space

The standard dimensions of the Wood fit many salons; still, if you have a particular spot in mind, such as a narrow corridor, a corner or a window bay, you can ask for the size to be changed. With a request like this, giving us the width and depth of the spot and any fixed obstacles around it makes our job easier.

## During treatments

The table is also a suitable work surface for long sessions such as nail extensions. Technician and guest sit opposite each other; supplies can be spread towards the two ends, leaving the middle clear for the guest's hands.
