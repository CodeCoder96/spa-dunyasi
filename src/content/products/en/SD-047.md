## A natural look next to wet areas

Sauna cabins, hammam entrances and relaxation lounges are usually designed around timber, stone and warm light. A metal appliance placed in that setting can look out of step. Wood solves this with its timber body, so it can sit in a towel service corner after the sauna, on a side table beside the loungers or at the door of a massage room and still look like part of the décor.

If you are planning a hotel spa or a thermal facility, matching the timber tone to the sauna cladding, door frames or lounge furniture gives a coherent result.

## Same data as its sister models

The fittings and technical table of the Wood are identical to the other two cabinets in the family. Inner height is 25 cm, inner depth 18.5 cm and volume 23 litres; it connects to a 220-volt, 50-60 Hz supply. So the real question when choosing is not performance but how the room around it looks.

These questions help when deciding between the three:

- Will the unit stand where guests can see it?
- Are the surrounding furniture and finishes timber, or white and pale?
- Will you use several cabinets in one facility, perhaps with different bodies in different zones?

When a business builds a sauna, hammam or steam room, the towel warmer is often remembered only at the end.
