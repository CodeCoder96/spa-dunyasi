## For therapists who go to the guest

Some therapists work at the guest's home, hotel room or residence rather than running their own salon. Working this way, the equipment has to fit in a car, be carried up stairs and go up in a few simple steps. The portable massage table is a product type developed for exactly this need.

Hotels and resorts take the same approach for in-room massage: the table goes to the guest who cannot come to the spa, and it is packed away once the treatment is over. Villa rentals, wedding preparations, private parties and sports clubs often follow a similar pattern. In every case, what helps the therapist most is equipment that sets up securely, stays stable and lets the guest lie down comfortably.

## A second role inside the spa

A portable table is not only for mobile work. It is also handy when you open an extra treatment space on busy days, need a second bed for a couples package or set up a temporary layout for training sessions. Being able to put it away afterwards suits businesses that do not want to add permanent furniture to a room.
