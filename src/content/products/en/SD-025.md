## What a stone surface brings to a rest area

In a Turkish hammam, marble is the core material, from the central heated platform to the basins. A marble lounger carries that language into the rest area. A lounger in the same material as the hammam creates visual continuity between the wet zone and the rest zone, so moving from one to the other feels natural.

Unlike upholstered loungers, here the guest lies directly on stone. Marble is widely used in damp settings, which is why this lounger is chosen for hammam and steam room exits where guests arrive in wet bathrobes. Its low build makes it easy to sit down on and lie back.

## The heating option

Heating is listed as an option for the marble lounger in our catalogue. In the heated version the stone surface is kept warm, so a guest lying down in a cool lounge does not meet a cold surface. An unheated version can make sense inside the hammam or in other areas that are already warm.

Telling us early whether you want heating makes coordination with the team planning your building services easier. If you are ordering several loungers, they do not all need the same configuration: you might choose unheated loungers near the hammam and heated ones on the cooler side of the lounge.

## Trolley and project sizes

A trolley can be added next to the lounger as a surface for towels, slippers or drinks. As the dimensions can be adapted on request, a marble lounger can be sized to fit a hammam alcove, a built-in bench along a wall or a dedicated rest corner.
