## Where it sits in the Base series

Every bed in the Base family shares the same core: an MDF body, the lower compartment, a 10 cm cushion and the same list of extras. So rather than looking for a technical advantage, the better question with the Base X Cube is this: what line do your room's furniture and wall panels follow? The word "Cube" in the name refers to the form of the body. Put the product photos side by side with [Base X Square](urun:SD-006) and the other siblings, and pick the one that fits your room.

## Planning the room

The bed is 80 cm wide and 200 cm long. Leave enough space around it for the therapist to move freely on both sides. Because the body already has storage, you may not need a separate cabinet next to it, which frees up floor space in a compact room.

If the room is close to a hammam or other wet area, the water- and moisture-resistant MDF body is a real plus.

## Choosing colours and extras

To make the decision easier, start with how the room will be used:

- For body massage only, the standard build suits most rooms.
- If you also offer skincare, add the manual backrest lift.
- If atmosphere matters, look at the seven-colour LED therapy lighting.
- For heavy use, a protective cover and segmented cushion simplify daily care.

Then settle on the wood and upholstery colours and send us your choices.

If you are still undecided, a photo of the room and its measurements help a great deal. We can look together at how the bed will sit, which side the therapist will work from and how the storage will be reached. To compare it with other models, browse our [massage beds](kategori:masaj-yataklari).
