## What wenge brings to a room

The first thing you notice about Avant is its colour. Wenge is a timber tone that sits between deep brown and black, and in a dimly lit treatment room it lets the bed blend quietly into the space. Against pale walls it does the opposite and becomes the focal point. That makes Avant an easy match for spas whose interiors are built around natural materials and earthy shades.

## Upholstery for generous use of oil

Ayurvedic massage uses warm oil freely. On a bed with flat edges, some of that oil can run off the sides and onto the floor. On the Avant the edges of the upholstery are kept high, so the oil stays on the surface, the therapist can carry on without interruption and clean-up afterwards is easier to manage.

At its standard 50 cm height, guests can sit down and lie back without effort. If your therapists need a different working level, order the bed with the motor option.

## The venues it is aimed at

Wellness hotels with an Ayurveda menu, spas that focus on oil massage and salons offering Eastern massage styles are the kind of businesses Avant is designed for. Because the dimensions can be adapted, a room with an unusual shape is not a problem; share your wall measurements and we will look at it with you.

## Things to decide before you order

A few decisions will speed up your quote. First, choose between a fixed height and the motor option; if your therapists differ in height, the motor is usually worth it. Second, think about session length and how much oil you use, since a protective cover and a segmented cushion make the upholstery easier to look after. Finally, consider the lighting: the seven-colour LED therapy light works well with the dark wenge body.
