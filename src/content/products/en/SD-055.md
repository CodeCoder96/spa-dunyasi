## The warmth of timber in a wet room

Wet treatment rooms are usually finished in ceramic, mosaic or stone, which can make them feel cool. The Wood vichy shower balances that with timber. Its body is made from water- and moisture-resistant timber, so you do not have to give up a wooden look on a unit that is in constant contact with water.

The colour of the wood is chosen to order. Match it to cabin doors, the sauna front or the furniture in the rest area and your whole spa can share one consistent material palette. If you would rather have a stone-led look, the [mosaic and marble vichy shower](urun:SD-053) is the alternative.

## Workflow in the treatment room

In a vichy session the guest lies on the bed, the therapist starts the flow from the 6 heads above and guides the treatment. Because massage can be given under the rain shower, the unit works as a service station in its own right, not just as a rinsing point. Scheduling a scrub, a body mask and a massage one after another on the same bed means the guest never has to change rooms.

The optional water cushion is added to the surface the guest lies on. If you already know which treatments you will offer, mention them in your quote request so we can consider the options together.

A room with a vichy shower is usually planned with a shower, a rest corner and a towel cabinet. When guests can step straight from the wet area into a rest zone after their session, the room runs smoothly.

## Technical planning

The unit measures 95 × 215 cm and stands 80 cm high. When sizing the room, add space around these measurements for the therapist to move. The water supply, the drain position and a floor laid to fall towards the drain should all be settled before work begins.
