## Comfort shaped by the extras

Some loungers of the same size are completed with a trolley and a cover, but on the Uranus the extras are all about the cushion. The bolster set comes in two shapes, a cylinder and a half-moon. Guests can tuck them under the knees, into the small of the back or at neck level and arrange their own lying position. The segmented cushion is for operators who prefer an upholstered surface divided into sections rather than one single pad.

## From the massage room to the rest room

The room where a guest lies down with a tea after a massage or body treatment is really part of the treatment itself. Uranus is well suited to these rooms. At 50 cm high, a guest coming off the treatment couch can sit down without effort, and the 10 cm cushion over 32 DNS foam keeps longer rests comfortable.

The base is water- and moisture-resistant timber, so the lounger can also go in rooms next to a hammam or steam room exit.

A practical way to size such a room is to match the number of loungers to the number of treatments that finish at the same time. With two massage rooms, for example, having at least two loungers avoids guests waiting their turn. The 85 cm backrest also gives a comfortable posture when sitting up, so the room works for chatting as well as lying down.

## Colour and size decisions

Colour choice applies to the upholstery. If you are ordering bolsters too, include them when you plan the overall look of the loungers. Dimensions can be adapted on request, so send us your measurements if your room does not suit a standard layout.
