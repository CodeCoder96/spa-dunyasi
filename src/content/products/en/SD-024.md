## Mercury in a relaxation area

A quiet place to lie down after the hammam, sauna or a massage is a core part of any spa visit. Mercury is built for exactly that: a lounger that sits close to the floor. At 45 cm high, guests can sit down and get up easily, and the 200 cm length gives taller guests room to stretch out.

The manual backrest lifts to 35°. When a guest wants to read, have a tea or chat, they simply raise the back. With no motor or cables involved, you can place the lounger anywhere in the room.

## Materials and finish

The base is water- and moisture-resistant timber, and the upholstery combines 32 DNS foam with a leather finish over a 10 cm cushion. Timber and leather colours are made to order, so you can line up a row of loungers that matches your floor and wall tones.

## Extras and companions

Mercury can be ordered with a trolley and a protective leather cover. The trolley gives guests a surface for towels and personal items, and the cover helps protect the upholstery in busy rooms.
