## Who considers it

Spas whose massage menu includes warm-oil treatments or special rituals that use sand tend to keep a unit like this close to the therapist's work area. Having the oil or sand ready before the session starts helps the treatment run without interruptions.

Before choosing, it helps to ask yourself:

- Do you offer a treatment with sand, or do you only need to warm oil?
- Will the unit sit on a worktop or on a trolley?
- Will the same room also hold other electrical units, such as a stone heater or a towel warmer?

The answers make it clearer whether a sand and oil heater suits you, or whether a unit that warms stones and oil together makes more sense.
