## Which rooms suit a white metal body?

Surfaces set the tone of a treatment room. Where the worktops are white, the cupboards pale and the lighting bright, a dark timber cabinet can feel heavy. The Towel Warmer avoids that: its plain metal body and white finish let it merge with the furniture around it.

That is why the model is most often chosen for skincare cabins, manicure and pedicure stations, hair salons and beauty clinics with a light, uncluttered design. If you are fitting out a massage studio dominated by wood tones, have a look at the sister models with a timber door or a timber body, which share the same technical table.

## Planning capacity

The compartment holds 23 litres. How many towels fit depends on their weight and how you fold them; the catalogue figure is 100 to 120. Small hand towels rolled tightly mean more pieces, while folded face towels mean fewer.

If you know your daily number of sessions, working out what you need is simple. Multiply the towels used per treatment by the number of sessions; if the result exceeds the cabinet's capacity, plan for a second cabinet or a refill during the day.

## Setting it up

The unit weighs 6 kg and runs on 220 volts at 50-60 Hz. It can go on a worktop or a sturdy shelf. Leave clearance at the back for the cable and enough space in front to take towels out comfortably.
