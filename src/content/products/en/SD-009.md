## Upholstery and foam

In a busy salon, the part of a massage bed that wears hardest is the upholstery. A surface wiped after every session and in constant contact with oil and lotion should not look tired too soon. That is why Dynamic uses Vinleks. Beneath it, 30 DNS foam helps the 10 cm cushion hold its shape through the day.

The body is built from a water- and moisture-resistant timber. In rooms near wet areas, or where humidity runs high, this helps the woodwork keep its appearance.

## For salons with a full diary

When appointments run back to back, the gap between sessions is short. In that window the bed has to be cleaned, the linen changed and everything set for the next guest. The wipeable surface of the Dynamic, together with the optional protective cover, keeps that cycle manageable.

The face hole lets guests keep their head comfortable during face-down work on the back and shoulders. If you choose one of the motorised versions, therapists of different heights can share the same bed more easily.

## Colour and room styling

With colour options for both timber and upholstery, you can order every bed in your salon in one shade or set rooms apart with different colours. The seven-colour LED therapy light is there for venues that want to change the lighting mood.

When you ask for a quote, let us know how many rooms you are fitting out and which motor version you have in mind for each. If you are unsure about anything, describe your workflow and we will find the right configuration together.
