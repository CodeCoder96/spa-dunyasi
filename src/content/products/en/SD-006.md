## Fitting out more than one room

In a hotel spa, guests expect the same level of care as they move from one room to the next. Using the same series of bed throughout gives you that consistency and keeps the daily care routine simple. Because the colours on the Base X Square are set to order, you can have every bed in one shade or pick a different upholstery colour for a signature suite.

Write down what each room will be used for as you plan. The bed suits massage, body care, skincare and spa treatments, so the same model can take on different roles.

## Extras for everyday use

In a busy spa, keeping beds clean and tidy saves time:

- A protective cover shields the upholstery from oil and lotion marks.
- A segmented cushion lets each section be cleaned separately.
- A drawer adds extra order to the storage under the body.
- Bolsters (cylinder and half-moon) support guests in different positions.

Heating (220V/180W) and LED therapy lighting can also be added bed by bed.

## When to choose the Square

Dimensions and materials are the same across the Base series in our catalogue. The reason to choose the Square is that the look of its body fits your interior design. Compare the photos with the [Base X Cube](urun:SD-004), and if you cannot decide, send us photos of your rooms.

## Preparing a project list

For a multi-room project, the quickest route to a quote is a clear list. On separate lines, note each room's purpose, the extras it needs and the colour you prefer. Add the room sizes and whether any are close to wet areas. With that in hand, we can bring every bed into a single quote and tell you where an extra will genuinely earn its place.
