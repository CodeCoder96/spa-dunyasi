## Why height adjustment matters

Manicure tables usually have a fixed height. The person sitting at one, however, has their own height, arm length and way of sitting. On a fixed chair these differences show up in elbow and shoulder position; over long treatments, people either lift their arms too high or lean forward.

The hydraulic mechanism closes that gap. The chair is raised or lowered to sit correctly against the table. In salons where different technicians or guests use the same chair through the day, each person can find their own level easily. If a table has been made to a non-standard size, an adjustable chair also lets you adapt seat height afterwards. That flexibility keeps a station coherent even when you buy furniture at different times or from different model lines.

## Materials and construction

The load-bearing part of the chair is a metal frame. The seat uses high-density moulded foam, and the outer surface is finished in faux leather. We suggest asking us when ordering which products are suitable for everyday cleaning of the upholstery; since manicures involve liquids, powders and acetone, this detail matters in how you run your salon.

The chair is 67 cm wide. When placing it at a table, leave enough space behind it to sit down and get up. If you are lining up several stations, allowing a walkway between chairs lets guests take their seats without disturbing each other. Deciding whether the chair goes on the guest side, the technician side or both also settles how many you need to order.
