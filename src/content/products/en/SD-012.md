## What sets the Line apart: the upholstery

When you compare several massage beds of the same size and similar build, the upholstery often decides it. Our catalogue entry for the Line names the upholstery material outright as Vinleks, laid over 30 DNS foam on a 10 cm cushion. On a bed that sees many guests a day, a surface you can wipe down and a colour that suits the rest of the room both matter. Because you choose the colour of the timber frame and of the upholstery separately, you can order the bed to match your existing cabin.

The frame is made from a water- and moisture-resistant grade of timber, which is worth keeping in mind for massage rooms where oils and creams are used and the air can be humid.

## Choosing the motor option for your work

The Line comes in three builds: fixed, single motor and two motors. A few questions help you decide which one fits:

- Will the bed be used only for classic body massage, or also for skincare work that needs the guest repositioned?
- Will therapists of different heights share the same bed?
- Is there a power socket within easy reach in the room?

A fixed bed may be all you need for straightforward use, and it lets you place the bed without thinking about where the socket is. For the motorised builds, confirm with us at the ordering stage which section each motor moves.

## Finishing touches for the cabin

The face hole lets the guest's head rest in a natural position during face-down back massage. Heating, a bolster and a protective cover shape daily use most directly, while a drawer keeps towels and oil bottles right under the bed.
