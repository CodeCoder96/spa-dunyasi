## A unit built from options

The Ozone Capsule does not come in one fixed configuration; it takes shape around the features you select. That is why the first step is to decide which services you want to offer.

### Heat

Infrared sauna and steam sauna are the two options that make up the heat side of the capsule. Some guests prefer dry heat and others a humid atmosphere, so choose according to where you want your service menu to lean.

### Atmosphere

Aromatherapy, the coloured light system and the music system set the mood of each session. Being able to choose the scent, the colour of the light and the music lets you design sessions with different themes on the same unit. You might, for instance, use bright colours and upbeat music in the morning and softer light with a calm playlist in the evening. You can also keep the configuration simple by choosing only the options you really need.

## Which businesses is it for?

For businesses without the room to build a separate sauna or steam room, the capsule format can be a practical answer. Beauty centres, boutique spa salons, hotel spas and the wellness areas of sports clubs can all consider adding it to their service menus. Placing the capsule close to a treatment room or rest area creates an easy flow, so guests can relax comfortably after their session.

If you are planning a full-size sauna or steam room instead, see our [sauna manufacturing](hizmet:sauna) and [steam room construction](hizmet:buhar-odasi) pages.
