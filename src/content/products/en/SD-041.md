## An answer for small spaces

Not every salon has a large manicure area. For one-room studios, hairdressers offering manicures in a corner, or people turning part of their home into a workspace, a full-size table can take up more room than it should. Mini is a model to consider in these situations.

A smaller table is about more than saving floor space. It leaves more room to move around, guests do not have to squeeze past on the way in and out, and the room looks tidier. A narrow stretch of wall between a door and a window, or the gap beside a column, can become usable with a table of the right size.

Mini also works as a second station. Salons that need an extra working spot next to the main table on busy days, or hotel spas wanting a small manicure point in a corner, can create a flexible layout with a compact table.

## Let's settle the size together

As there is no ready-made size for the Mini in the catalogue, we need to agree on dimensions before you order. To make that easier, we suggest sending us:

- The width and depth of the wall or area where the table will go
- How many people will sit at the table
- Devices you will use on it, such as a lamp or dryer
- Your preferred wood colour

With this information we can offer both a workable size and a price to match.

## Small table, full service

A compact table does not limit what you can offer. Mini is suitable for nail extension work too; by keeping supplies on a small trolley under or beside it, you can reserve the surface for the tools you are using at that moment. To see the other models, browse our [manicure](kategori:manikur) page.
