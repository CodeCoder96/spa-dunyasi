## When heating and a lifting backrest make sense

During a massage the guest is usually kept warm by the therapist's moving hands. During a facial or a mask, though, they lie still for a long time, and a cool surface can become uncomfortable. The heating available for Comfort is meant for quieter treatments like these. In air-conditioned rooms or in the colder season, a warm surface helps guests settle into the session.

The backrest lift comes into play when the guest needs to be semi-upright, for facials, make-up or a consultation. It is raised manually.

## Between skincare and massage

Thanks to its face hole, Comfort also works for face-down massage. In rooms that run facials in the morning and massage in the afternoon, one bed can do the job of two. The upholstery is Vinleks over 30 DNS foam, the cushion is 10 cm thick and the body is a water- and moisture-resistant timber.

## Sample configurations

- Beauty salon facial room: heating and manual backrest lift together.
- Massage studio: motorised body, heating optional.
- Hotel spa couples' suite: two beds in one colour, with bolsters and protective covers.

These are only suggestions, and we can work out the right mix for your room with you. When you get in touch, tell us how warm the room usually is and what position guests stay in during sessions; it helps us judge whether heating and the backrest lift are worth adding.

If you only offer massage and need neither heating nor a lifting backrest, [Classic](urun:SD-007) is also worth a look.
