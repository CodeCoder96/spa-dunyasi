## A bed for the head spa room

Head massage appears on many spa menus as a service in its own right. During the treatment the therapist works from a fixed position at the guest's head, often seated or standing, while the guest lies on their back. This bed is designed around that way of working. Oils and hair care products are part of the treatment, so the surface needs to be easy to clean, which is why antibacterial upholstery was chosen.

## Materials and storage

Beneath the upholstery is 28 DNS foam, and the cushion is 10 cm thick. The body is made from water- and moisture-resistant MDF, which matters in rooms where hair products are used and where the bed sits near a basin or shower head.

The lower part of the body has space for a massage pillow, oil, towels and peshtemals, so the therapist can reach supplies without leaving their position during a session. The upholstery colour is chosen to order.

## Planning the room

When setting up a head spa room, leave enough working space at the head end of the bed for the therapist. You may also want a towel warmer and a product shelf in the room; our [spa equipment checklist](rehber:spa-ekipman-listesi) is a handy reference while you plan.

The extras include heating (220V/180W), a bolster, a protective cover, seven-colour LED therapy lighting and a segmented cushion. LED lighting is worth considering in head spa rooms that aim for a dim, calm atmosphere.
