## Where Light fits best

In hotel spas, the lounge where guests wait after the hammam or sauna often has to hold a lot of loungers in limited space. The 65 cm width of the Light helps you fit more of them into that kind of layout. A 50 cm height and an 85 cm backrest make it easy for guests to settle in and lean back.

Under the upholstery sit 32 DNS foam and a 10 cm cushion. Because the base is water- and moisture-resistant timber, the loungers can go in areas used by guests arriving in damp bathrobes: indoor pool surrounds at sports clubs, rest corridors in thermal hotels and relaxation rooms at day spas are all suitable spots.

## Planning a row

If you are lining Light loungers along a long wall or on both sides of a pool, it is worth thinking about the following in advance:

- Leave enough space between loungers for a member of staff carrying towels to pass.
- If you want a trolley beside every lounger, or one for every two, include that in your quantities.
- Choose the upholstery colour to suit the floor and wall finishes. A single colour gives a calm row, while alternating two colours looks livelier.
- For heavily used lounges, budget for the protective leather cover from the start.
