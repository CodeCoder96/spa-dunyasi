## Why Thai massage needs its own bed

In Thai massage the guest lies fully clothed on a surface close to the floor, while the therapist kneels on the bed and moves beside and around them. Stretches and changes of position are frequent in this technique, so a standard massage bed is too narrow and too high. What it calls for is a wide, low, square surface.

The 200 × 200 cm size of the Thai massage bed in our catalogue gives the guest room to lie lengthways or across, and leaves the therapist space to work from every side. Its 350 kg load capacity is the figure to bear in mind for a treatment where two people are on the bed at the same time.

## With or without a base

The bed is made in two ways:

- **With a base:** the cushion sits on a timber base made from a water- and moisture-resistant grade of wood. Businesses that want a permanent, decorative look in the cabin tend to choose this version.
- **Without a base:** it lies directly on the floor and folds in half when needed. It suits businesses that use the room for different treatments during the day or want to move the bed around.

## Fit the size to your space

Being able to adapt the dimensions matters when you are making room in an existing building. A different size can be set by taking into account the cabin walls, the direction the door opens and the space the therapist needs to move around the bed. If you are planning treatments for two guests at once, we can also look at how much room you need to fit two beds.
