## For businesses with more than one cabin

When you plan a hotel spa or a day spa, not every room does the same job. A fixed bed may be enough in a room used for classic body massage, while a skincare cabin needs the guest repositioned during treatment. Space lets you handle that difference within one model: the look and size stay the same in every room, and only the motor build changes to suit the work.

This approach has two practical benefits. First, guests see a consistent interior whichever room they walk into. Second, therapists moving between rooms find the same bed size, the same cushion feel and the same face hole layout they are used to.

## Choose the colour to suit the space

Our catalogue entry for the Space states that colours for the timber and upholstery are offered to order. Lighter tones can work for a bright, airy room, darker tones for softly lit cabins; we will share the colours available when you ask. Before deciding, take the room's lighting and floor material into account.

## What to prepare before asking for a quote

To speed up your quote, you can send the following together:

1. The total number of beds and the treatments planned for each room
2. Fixed, single-motor or two-motor for each bed
3. The extras you want: heating, bolster, protective cover, LED lighting, drawer, segmented cushion
4. Your colour preference for the timber and upholstery
