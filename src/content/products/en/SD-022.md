## A backrest that sets it apart

When choosing a lounger, most operators look at the length first. With Evony, the measurement that matters most is the backrest. It reaches 85 cm high, so a guest lying down has a clear surface to rest their head and shoulders on. For guests who want to read, sip a tea or watch the pool, this profile is more practical than a flat bed.

The 50 cm height makes it easy to sit down and get up again, and the 195 cm length lets an adult guest stretch their legs out fully.

## Planning a space around upholstery colour

On the Evony, colour is chosen through the upholstery. That is useful wherever several loungers stand side by side. You might keep a whole row in one shade that picks up your floor and wall tones, or use different colours to mark out zones, such as one shade by the pool and another in the indoor rest lounge.

The base is made from water- and moisture-resistant timber, which matters in spots close to a pool, a hammam exit or a shower area.

## Who is it for?

Evony fits hotel spas, the rest areas of thermal resorts, pool halls in sports clubs and the waiting lounges of beauty centres: anywhere guests sit for a while before or after a session. Because the backrest is tall, the lounger works as a seat as well as a bed, so one piece of furniture can cover both waiting and resting and you may need fewer armchairs in the room.

## Trolley and cover

Two extras are available for the Evony:

- **Trolley:** a small surface next to the lounger for towels, glasses and personal items.
- **Protective leather cover:** helps the upholstery last longer in rooms with heavy guest traffic.
