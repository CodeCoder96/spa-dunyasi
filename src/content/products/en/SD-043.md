## Why a timber-fronted cabinet?

In a spa or massage studio the towel warmer usually stands where guests can see it: on the therapist's worktop, on a side table next to the bed or behind the reception desk. A dark timber door makes the unit read as a piece of furniture rather than an appliance. In rooms with wood panelling, natural stone floors or warm-toned textiles, it blends in instead of standing out.

A metal-bodied cabinet looks more technical; this model suits venues that want a warmer atmosphere. If you cannot decide between the two, send us a photo of the spot where it will go and we will talk it through with you.

## Loading it well

The job of the cabinet is to warm the towels for the next treatment and keep them warm through the session. With the inner height and depth in mind, you can arrange towels rolled or folded, leaving gaps so warm air can move between them. Loading only what you expect to use that day stops towels being heated and cooled over and over.

The unit is light enough for a worktop, a shelf or a sturdy side table. Leave room for the door to open fully and place it close to a socket; both make daily use simpler.
