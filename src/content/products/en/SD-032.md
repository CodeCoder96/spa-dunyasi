## Where it fits

Brand suits treatment rooms in beauty clinics and hotel spas where the day is spent on facials, post-analysis skincare and hand or foot care. It sits between a chair and a bed: guests can be seated upright or lie fully flat.

## Why electric adjustment matters

A frequent complaint about treatment chairs is that the therapist cannot reach a comfortable working height. On the Brand, height, backrest and footrest move independently from the hand control. The beautician can lower the chair to work seated, raise it to help a guest stand up and change the backrest angle for each step of a treatment.

The frame is built to stay steady while you work, which matters during detailed facial work. The face hole is ready for any treatment that needs the guest lying face down.

## Extras

Heating keeps guests comfortable in cool rooms. A bolster supports the knees or neck. The 4th motor option adds the Trendelenburg position. Choose the extras you need and pick an upholstery colour to match your cabin.
