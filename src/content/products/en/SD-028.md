## Who needs the extra width?

A few centimetres can noticeably change how a guest feels on a lounger. The 75 cm width of the Odessa gives more room to anyone who likes to rest their arms at their sides or turn onto one side. It is a sensible choice wherever comfort per guest matters more than the number of loungers:

- VIP or suite rest rooms in hotel spas
- Rest corners in private couples' spa suites
- Calm lounges in boutique spa and wellness centres
- Indoor pool edges furnished with only a few loungers

## Layout considerations

A wider lounger affects how many fit along one wall. When planning, first decide how many guests you want to host at once, then how much floor area you want per lounger. In larger lounges, combining Odessa with a narrower model such as [Mercury](urun:SD-024) is a good middle ground. Setting aside one corner for the wider loungers lets you meet different guest expectations in the same room.

## Materials, colour and extras

The base is made from water- and moisture-resistant timber, and the upholstery combines 32 DNS foam with a 10 cm cushion. Colour can be chosen for both the timber and the leather, so you can order loungers to suit the tones of your space. The backrest is raised by hand to 35°, letting guests choose between lying flat and sitting half upright.

A trolley and a protective leather cover are available as extras. A trolley beside a wide lounger keeps a guest's phone, book or glass of tea within easy reach.
