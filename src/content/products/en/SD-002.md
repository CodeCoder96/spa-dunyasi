## Oil, upholstery and edges

In Ayurveda sessions, oil is used far more freely than in an everyday massage. The surface of the bed is not just somewhere to lie; it also collects the oil. The upholstery on this bed was prepared with that in mind. Because the edges stand higher, the oil gathers towards the centre and the therapist does not need to wipe the sides after every stroke.

It also lowers the chance of a slippery floor, which matters for guests and staff alike in rooms where oil sessions run all day.

## Extras that follow the session

As standard the bed is 50 cm high. For therapists who stand through long sessions, the motor option makes the height adjustable. The other extras depend on how you run your treatments:

- Heating keeps the surface from feeling cool when warm oil is applied.
- A bolster supports the guest's knees or ankles.
- A drawer keeps oil bowls and small towels within reach.
- A protective cover and a segmented cushion make daily care of the upholstery simpler.
