## The heart of a waxing station

In a beauty salon that offers waxing, the pace of work depends largely on whether the wax is ready at the right temperature. The catalogue describes this unit as a double wax heater with electric temperature control. We are happy to confirm how the double layout and pots are arranged before you order.

The electric control is there so you can manage the consistency of the wax to your liking. Different brands of wax work at different temperatures, so set it according to the instructions for the product you use.

## Materials and size

The pots are made of stainless steel and aluminium. A 12 cm pot diameter gives a practical capacity for the wax tins and loose wax commonly used in salons. The unit is rated at 200W, and its compact build means it fits easily on a manicure table, a side table next to the waxing bed or a trolley.

The catalogue lists the voltage as 110V/220-250V and the frequency as 50-60 Hz. If you are ordering for a branch abroad, simply tell us the mains values of that country.

## Planning the workspace

When setting up a waxing station, place the heater within reach of the treatment bed but where a client's arm will not knock it. Keeping the cable away from walkways and standing the unit on a flat, firm surface makes daily work tidier.
