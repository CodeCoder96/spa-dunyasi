## Four things to settle before you order

Answering four questions before ordering Venus helps you land on the right configuration and lets us prepare your quote faster.

### Where will the lounger stand?

The Venus base is water- and moisture-resistant timber. As well as enclosed rest lounges, it can be placed in areas near a hammam, sauna or pool exit.

### How many do you need?

At 65 cm wide, several loungers line up easily along one wall. The quantity affects both the layout and the overall quote.

### Which upholstery colour?

The colour choice applies to the upholstery. Send us the colours of your existing furniture and textiles and we can agree on a suitable shade together.

### What cushion arrangement?

The standard upholstery is 32 DNS foam with a 10 cm cushion. For a surface split into sections, add the segmented cushion; for extra support, add the bolster set.
