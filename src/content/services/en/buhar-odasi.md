## Why a steam room calls for specialist work

In a steam room, moisture is in constant contact with the walls, ceiling and floor. The layers beneath the finished surface therefore decide whether the room stays trouble-free over time. A gently sloping ceiling lets condensation run down the walls rather than drip onto bathers. The fall of the floor towards the drain, the shape of the seating and the seal around the door are all part of the same plan. Settling these details at the design stage reduces the need for corrections later.

## Design points we agree together

In design meetings for a steam room, we work through these points in turn:

- **Seating:** a single bench along one wall, two facing rows or seating that wraps around a corner.
- **Door:** whether it is glazed, how visible the room is from outside and which way the door opens.
- **Finishes:** whether mosaic, stone or ceramic-look surfaces suit the rest of the space, and which colours to use.
- **Light and atmosphere:** decorative lighting such as a starry ceiling, colour-changing lights or a simple, dim setting.
- **Ease of use:** where steam enters relative to the seats, where the control panel goes and access for cleaning.

## Commercial projects

In spas and hotels the steam room usually sits within a wet area alongside a hammam and sauna. So that guests can move easily between these rooms and reach the showers and rest area, we plan the layout across the whole space. In busy venues, cleaning and ventilation routines also need thinking about from the outset. For the rest area outside, the [Moon Relaxation Lounger](urun:SD-026) with its hand-lifted backrest, a [Towel Warmer](urun:SD-046) and the [Towel Cooler](urun:SD-044) can be added to the same project, and a [Vichy shower](urun:SD-053) can be planned in a separate room for wet treatments.

## Home projects

At home, a steam room is usually built inside a large bathroom or in an area next to it. In that case the shower and steam room can even share one enclosure. With limited space, the shape of the seating and the direction the door opens become especially important. We choose the finish colours and lighting with you so they sit well with the rest of the bathroom.

## Easy to use and easy to care for

How well a steam room keeps working depends on how easy it is to use each day. Large surfaces with few grout lines, seating with rounded corners and a floor that drains clearly all shorten cleaning time. Leaving service access to the steam generator and placing the control panel where users can see it are details we build into the design. For businesses, we talk through closing routines for staff at handover.

## What to have ready before you start

Before our first conversation, we suggest gathering the following:

- **Measurements:** the width, length and ceiling height of the room or the area you have set aside, and the position of doors and windows.
- **The space:** whether it is new or an existing bathroom or room, where water and drainage lines run, the electrical supply and ventilation options.
- **Intended use:** who will use the room, how many people at a time, and whether it will run on its own or alongside a hammam or sauna.
