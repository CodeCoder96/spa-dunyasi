## Fitting out or refreshing a spa

Choosing equipment for a spa, a hotel wellness area or a beauty clinic raises the same questions every time: which bed suits which treatment, which model fits the room, and is an electric bed really necessary? We have built our product pages to answer exactly that, with the dimensions, extras and typical uses set out clearly for each item.

## What each area needs

Spa equipment usually falls into three zones. Treatment rooms need a massage bed or treatment chair, often with a trolley and a stool; relaxation areas need loungers where guests can lie down; wet areas need water-based equipment such as a vichy shower. Towel warmers, sterilisers and hot stone heaters support all three.

Our categories follow the same logic. Among the massage beds you will find fixed, single-motor, two-motor and three-motor versions, while the relaxation loungers come in timber, leather, marble and mosaic finishes. Comparing the dimensions on each product page with your own room makes choosing much easier.
