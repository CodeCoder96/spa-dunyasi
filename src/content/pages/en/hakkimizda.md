## What we do

We present and sell equipment made by Spa World, from massage beds and relaxation loungers to treatment and pedicure chairs and towel warmers. Every product on this site is manufactured by Spa World. Each product page sets out the dimensions, extras and typical uses, and the product code gives us a shared reference when you get in touch.

You can find more about Spa World at [spaworld.com.tr](https://www.spaworld.com.tr).

## Who we work with

Our customers are mainly spas, hotels, beauty clinics, physiotherapy practices and massage studios. For sauna, hammam and pool projects we work with homes as well as businesses. The [services](sayfa:hizmetler) page explains how a project runs.

## Warranty and delivery

All our products come with a 5-year warranty. Please contact us for the warranty terms and coverage.

We deliver across Türkiye and abroad. The delivery plan is agreed with you based on the product, the extras chosen and the order quantity.

## How we work

Prices vary with the model, extras, colours and quantity, so we do not publish them on the site. Add the products you are interested in to your quote list and send it on WhatsApp or by email, and we will reply with a price prepared for your needs. Sharing your room size, the treatments you offer and the quantities you need helps us suggest the right products and extras.

With over 5 years of experience in the spa and beauty sector, we are happy to talk through any questions you have while you decide.

## Why we use product codes

Every product on our site has a code starting with SD. It links Spa World's product names to our catalogue pages and avoids mix-ups in messages: quote the code and it is clear which model, extras and colour we are talking about.
