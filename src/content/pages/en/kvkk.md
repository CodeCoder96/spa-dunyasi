This notice is provided under Article 10 of the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (the "Law") for the spadunyasi.com.tr website and the contact channels started through it.

## Data controller

Spa World — Fındıklı, Yaseminler Sokağı No: 7 A, 34854 Maltepe/İstanbul, Türkiye — info@spaworld.com.tr

## Personal data processed

The website has no form that collects personal data. When you contact us by WhatsApp, phone or email, we may process the information you share in that conversation, such as your name, phone number, email address and the content of your message.

## Purposes

Your personal data is processed to answer your product and service requests, prepare quotes, carry out orders and projects, and meet our legal obligations.

## Collection method and legal basis

Data is collected through electronic channels that you start yourself (WhatsApp, phone, email). The legal bases under Article 5 of the Law are that processing is directly related to concluding or performing a contract, that it is necessary for the data controller to meet a legal obligation, and the data controller's legitimate interest, provided it does not harm your fundamental rights and freedoms.

## Transfers

Within the limits of these purposes and in line with the legislation, your data may be shared with our business partners, service providers and authorised public bodies. When WhatsApp (Meta) and Google services are used, data may be processed outside Türkiye; see the [privacy policy](sayfa:gizlilik) for details.

## Your rights

Under Article 11 of the Law you have the right to learn whether your data is processed; to request information about it; to learn the purpose of processing and whether it is used accordingly; to know the third parties in Türkiye or abroad to whom it is transferred; to ask for incomplete or inaccurate data to be corrected; to ask for deletion or destruction under the conditions in the Law; to ask for these actions to be notified to third parties; to object to a result against you arising solely from automated analysis; and to claim compensation for damage caused by unlawful processing.

## How to apply

Send your request by email to info@spaworld.com.tr or in writing to the address above. Requests are concluded within the period set out in the Law.
