This policy explains how personal data is handled when you visit spadunyasi.com.tr. Detailed information on the processing of personal data is given in the [personal data notice](sayfa:kvkk).

## Data controller

The data controller is Spa World. Address: Fındıklı, Yaseminler Sokağı No: 7 A, 34854 Maltepe/İstanbul, Türkiye. Email: info@spaworld.com.tr.

## No data-collecting forms

The site has no form asking for your name, phone number or email. Contact happens through a WhatsApp message, phone call or email that you start yourself. Information you send us through these channels is used only to answer your request and prepare a quote.

## Browser storage

The site keeps some preferences only in your own browser; they are never sent to our server:

- **sd_quote_v1 (functional):** stores the product codes and quantities in your quote list. It is removed when you clear the list or your browser data.
- **sd_consent_v1:** stores your choice about analytics cookies. It is created only if the site uses analytics and asks for your choice.

## Analytics

Google Analytics runs only if the site administrator has enabled it and only after you give consent. Until you consent, no measurement request is sent to Google. The current status and cookie list are in the [cookie policy](sayfa:cerez).

## Third-party services and international transfers

- **WhatsApp (Meta):** when you click a WhatsApp link, the conversation takes place on the WhatsApp service, provided by Meta, and data may be processed outside Türkiye.
- **Google:** the "Open in Maps" and "Get directions" links open Google Maps. If analytics is enabled and you consent, Google Analytics is used. Data may be processed outside Türkiye as part of Google's services.

These services are governed by their own privacy policies.

## Your rights

Your rights regarding personal data and how to exercise them are described in the [personal data notice](sayfa:kvkk).
