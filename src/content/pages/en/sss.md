faq:
  - q: What is the product code that starts with SD?
    a: Every product on the site has its own code, for example SD-013. Quoting it in your message makes clear which product you mean and helps us prepare your quote faster.
  - q: Can products be made to custom dimensions?
    a: Some products can be adapted to custom dimensions; this is noted under "Equipment and features" on the product page. Send us the size of your space and we will look at the options with you.
  - q: Can I choose the colours?
    a: Many products offer a choice of colours for the timber and upholstery. The product page states which parts can be ordered in your colour.
  - q: How do I choose the extras?
    a: Each product page lists the extras that apply to that product, such as heating, a bolster, a protective cover or a drawer. Simply mention the ones you want in your price request.
  - q: How is the delivery plan decided?
    a: It depends on the product, the extras chosen and the order quantity. We agree the delivery plan with you when your order is confirmed.
  - q: Can I order from outside Türkiye?
    a: Yes. We deliver abroad as well as across Türkiye. You can contact us in English on WhatsApp or by email.
  - q: Where can I find the warranty terms?
    a: All products come with a 5-year warranty. For coverage and terms, contact us by phone, WhatsApp or email.
  - q: What do your project services cover?
    a: We work on sauna, hammam and pool projects for businesses and homes, from design and manufacturing to installation and handover. See the [services](sayfa:hizmetler) page for details.
  - q: Do I need an account to keep a quote list?
    a: No. The quote list is stored in your browser and stays with you as you move between pages. When it is ready, send it on WhatsApp or by email in one tap.
  - q: How do I decide which product suits my business?
    a: Our [guides](sayfa:rehber) explain how to choose a massage bed, a relaxation lounger and a treatment chair. If you are still unsure, send us your room size and the treatments you offer.
