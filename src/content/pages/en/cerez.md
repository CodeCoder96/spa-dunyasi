This policy explains what the spadunyasi.com.tr website stores in your browser. General information on personal data is in the [privacy policy](sayfa:gizlilik).

## Essential and functional storage

- **sd_quote_v1:** keeps the product codes and quantities in your quote list in your browser's local storage (localStorage). It is never sent to the server and is removed when you clear the list or your browser data.

This storage is needed for the quote list to work and does not require consent.
