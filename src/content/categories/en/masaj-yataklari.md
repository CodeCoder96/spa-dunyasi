## What to look for in a massage bed

Most massage beds in our range have an 80 × 200 cm surface and a 350 kg load capacity. What really separates the models is the base design, the number of motors and the available extras.

**Motors:** Models such as [Classic](urun:SD-007), [Trend](urun:SD-020) and [Space](urun:SD-018) can be built fixed, with one motor or with two. [Lux Soft](urun:SD-013), [Soft](urun:SD-017) and [Nice](urun:SD-014) have three motors, so height, backrest and footrest are all set from the hand control.

**Base and materials:** The Base series stands out for its water- and moisture-resistant MDF body and a lower compartment for a pillow, oils, towels and peshtemals. On timber-framed models, the timber and upholstery colours are made to order.

**Beds for specific treatments:** [Ayurveda](urun:SD-002) and [Avant](urun:SD-001) have raised upholstery edges for oil-based work, the [Thai Massage Bed](urun:SD-019) suits floor-style Thai massage, and the [Head Massage Bed](urun:SD-011) is built for head and scalp treatments. For mobile therapists there is also a [portable massage table](urun:SD-016).

## What sets the price of a massage bed?

There is no single price for a massage bed. The model, a fixed or electric base, the number of motors, the extras you add and the timber and upholstery colours all play a part, and ordering several beds at once changes the figure too. Of the 21 massage beds in our range, seven can be built fixed, with one motor or with two, and three come with three motors. Every product is manufactured by Spa World and carries a 5-year warranty. We do not publish prices on the site; instead we send a current price on WhatsApp or by phone for the configuration you choose.
