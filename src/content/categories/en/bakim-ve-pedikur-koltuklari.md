## Pedicure chairs with a foot spa basin

[Light](urun:SD-033), [Spa](urun:SD-036), [Spa Life](urun:SD-035) and [Wood](urun:SD-037) share one set of dimensions: 190 cm wide, 140 cm long and 110 cm high. All four have a foot spa basin and a height adjustment that helps the therapist work comfortably. Because the technical data is the same, the choice between them usually comes down to the style of your salon and the timber and upholstery colours you prefer. Dimensions can also be adapted, which helps when a station has to fit a tight corner.

## Electric treatment and podology chairs

The [Brand beauty treatment chair](urun:SD-032) and the [Podology Care Chair](urun:SD-034) are 77 cm wide and 180 cm long, with a 200 kg load capacity. Height, backrest and footrest are all adjusted electrically from the hand control, and the frame stays steady while you work. Extras include a face hole, heating, a bolster and a fourth motor. You choose the upholstery colour.

## Which group suits your business?

In salons that focus on foot care, the pedicure chair is the centre of the station. Rooms used for facials, brow and lash work or podology benefit more from an electric chair that lets the therapist change the client's position. If you plan to use both in one centre, our [pedicure chair buying guide](rehber:pedikur-koltugu-secimi) goes into more detail.

## Layout and colour matching

Pedicure chairs have a wide footprint, so leave room in front for the therapist's stool and at the side for a trolley. For electric chairs, decide early where the socket and the hand control cable will go. In both groups the upholstery can be ordered to match the rest of your furniture.
