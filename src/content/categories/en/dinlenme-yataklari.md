## Three families by frame

**Timber frames:** Every model in this group is built from a water- and moisture-resistant timber, with 32 DNS foam and a 10 cm cushion. [Evony](urun:SD-022), [Light](urun:SD-023), [Uranus](urun:SD-030) and [Venus](urun:SD-031) offer a 65 × 195 cm surface and an 85 cm backrest. [Mercury](urun:SD-024), [Moon](urun:SD-026) and [Odessa](urun:SD-028) are 200 cm long and have a backrest you lift by hand. At 75 cm wide, Odessa gives guests a roomier surface than the others.

**Stone and mosaic surfaces:** The [Marble Relaxation Lounger](urun:SD-025) and the [Mosaic Relaxation Lounger](urun:SD-027) suit venues that want to carry the look of a hammam into the rest area. The mosaic version comes in a wide palette of glass mosaic colours.

**For outdoor spaces:** The [Rattan Sun Lounger](urun:SD-029) works indoors and outdoors. Its surface coating has a UV filter against sunlight, it copes with both heat and cold, and a side table can be ordered with it for a poolside or terrace.

## Extras and colours

Evony, Light, Mercury, Moon and Odessa can be ordered with a trolley and a protective leather cover. Uranus and Venus offer a bolster and a segmented cushion instead. On the timber models you choose the upholstery colour, and on several of them the timber colour as well.

## Planning the room

When working out how many loungers you need, allow for the walkway between them and for any side table or trolley next to each one, not just the lounger itself. Models with a lifting backrest let guests sit up to read or have a cup of tea; for a room meant purely for lying down, the other models do the job just as well. Rattan is the natural choice for a pool deck or terrace, while timber, marble or mosaic frames fit indoor and humid areas.
