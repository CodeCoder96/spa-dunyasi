## Vichy showers

The [Mosaic and Marble Vichy Shower](urun:SD-053) and the [Wood Vichy Shower](urun:SD-055) are made to the same size: 95 cm wide, 215 cm long and 80 cm high. Each has 6 shower heads, and the treatment takes place under the rain shower. The frame uses a water- and moisture-resistant timber whose colour you can choose, and a water cushion can be added.

The real difference is the surface. A hammam finished in marble or mosaic calls for the stone model, while the Wood version keeps a timber-led spa consistent.

## Ozone capsule

The [Ozone Capsule](urun:SD-054) is configured through its equipment options. You can pick from infrared sauna, steam sauna, aromatherapy, a coloured light system and a music system. As the source has no technical table, we share size and connection details on request.

## Plan the wet area as a whole

A Vichy shower or capsule is usually part of the same project as a hammam or steam room, so plumbing, drainage and floor finishes should be planned with it. If you are building the space from scratch, our [hammam construction](hizmet:hamam) and [steam room construction](hizmet:buhar-odasi) pages walk through the process.

## Who uses this equipment?

Vichy showers are found mostly in hotel spas offering hammam and body rituals and in beauty centres with a wet treatment room. The ozone capsule brings several options into one unit for centres that want to design different experiences. Before ordering, check the width of the room entrance and how the unit will reach its final position.
