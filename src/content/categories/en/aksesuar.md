## Inside the treatment room

The [Bolster](urun:SD-057) is made in neck, cylinder, triangle, chest support and half-moon shapes, and is used to support the client's position on the massage bed. [Trolley](urun:SD-059) options include a beautician trolley, a Thai trolley and numbered models.

For the therapist, the [Stool](urun:SD-062) runs on castors and comes with or without a backrest, as well as in saddle and flat seat versions. Its height range is 50-65 cm and it is offered in a choice of colours. For close work such as facials and manicures, the [Floor-Standing Magnifier Lamp](urun:SD-056) has a five-leg base, 90 LEDs and an adjustable light level.

## Beyond the treatment room

[Massage Oil](urun:SD-060) is offered in rose, orange, jasmine, green tea, chamomile, lavender and sandalwood. In the relaxation area, the [Lounge Side Table](urun:SD-058) sits beside the loungers; the source gives no dimensions for it.

For venues with a pool or gym, the [Swimsuit Dryer](urun:SD-061) has a stainless steel body with PE plastic fittings. It handles bikinis, shorts and swimsuits and weighs 25 kg.

## Do not leave gaps

Bolsters, stools and trolleys are easy to forget when a new room is being fitted out, and without them a therapist starts work short of equipment. Draw up a list per room and order these items together with the bed. Where colour is an option, match the tones to your beds and chairs. Keeping several oil scents to hand also lets guests make a small choice before their treatment.
