## Tables

[Glass](urun:SD-038), [Light](urun:SD-039) and [Wood](urun:SD-042) are built to the same size: 130 cm long, 55 cm deep and 82 cm high. All three are suitable for nail extension work, and the timber can be finished in the colour you want. With a shared technical sheet, the decision mostly rests on your interior and the look you prefer.

The [Mini Manicure Table](urun:SD-041) has the same features, but the source gives no size table for it. If you are considering this model, we suggest confirming its dimensions with us first.

## Manicure chair

The [Manicure Chair](urun:SD-040) has a metal frame, high-density moulded foam and faux leather upholstery. It is 67 cm wide, and its hydraulic mechanism moves the seat between 77-92 cm. The upholstery colour can be chosen to go with your tables.

## Completing the station

A manicure station rarely stops at a table and a chair. A [stool on castors](urun:SD-062) for the technician, a [floor-standing magnifier lamp](urun:SD-056) for detailed work and a trolley for supplies can all go on the same order, which makes it easier to keep colours and sizes consistent.

## Choosing for your salon layout

In a small boutique salon with a single table, the table itself is on show, so pick a timber colour that works with your walls and floor. Centres with several stations often repeat one model to keep the room looking orderly. Because dimensions can be adapted, tables can also be made to line up along a wall.
