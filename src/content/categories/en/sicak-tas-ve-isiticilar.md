## Stone heaters

The [Pan Stone Heater](urun:SD-050) and the [Oil and Stone Heater](urun:SD-052) have the same body: 40 cm long, 25 cm wide and 12 cm high. Both heat to 55-80° and run on 220V/180W. Where they part ways is scope. Pan warms stones and massage balls, while the second model warms oil as well. If you want one unit to prepare both stones and oil in the room, the second model is the more practical choice.

## Volcanic stone set

The [Volcanic Stone Set](urun:SD-051) is made up of stones in different shapes and sizes. It weighs 8 kg net and includes a wooden spoon. Ordering it together with a heater makes it easier to plan how the set and the unit will be used side by side.

## For oil, sand and wax

There is no technical data in the source for the [Sand and Oil Heater](urun:SD-049), so we share details on request. The [Wax Heater](urun:SD-048) is a double wax heater with stainless steel and aluminium pots, electric temperature control, 200W of power and a 12 cm pot diameter. It is a good fit for beauty salons that want a separate waxing station.

## Setting up the room

Placing the heater on a trolley or worktop within the therapist's reach, close to the massage bed, keeps the treatment flowing. Keep it near a socket and route the cable away from walkways. If several rooms offer hot stone treatments, decide early whether each will have its own heater or share a preparation point; that settles the quantity to order.
