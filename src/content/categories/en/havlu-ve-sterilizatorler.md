## Heating cabinets

The [Towel Warmer Cabinet](urun:SD-043), the [Towel Warmer](urun:SD-046) and the [Wood Towel Warmer](urun:SD-047) all use a cabinet with a UV filter and a heating element. Their technical sheets are identical: a 23-litre volume, a weight of 6 kg and a 220-volt supply. That leaves appearance as the deciding factor, so think about how each will look at reception or in the treatment room. The Wood model is the one to consider for a room with a lot of timber.

## Steriliser cabinet

The [Steriliser Cabinet](urun:SD-045) shares the same UV filter and heating element. Where it differs is volume: the technical sheet lists three options, 5, 16 and Mini. We can help you match one to your daily towel use.

## Towel cooler

The [Towel Cooler](urun:SD-044) uses compressor cooling. It has a sensor-activated LED interior light, an automatic timer and automatic defrost. The interior and door shelves are adjustable and the door can be reversed. Placed next to a warmer, it keeps both hot and cold towels within reach whatever the order of the treatment.

## How many units do you need?

That depends on how many rooms run at once and where towels are prepared. A warmer in each room lets the therapist work without leaving it; with a central preparation point, fewer units may do. A cooler usually sits in a shared spot. Send us your floor plan and we will make a suggestion. You can read more in our [towel warmer guide](rehber:havlu-isitici-kullanimi).

## Towel warmer prices

The price varies with the model, the volume, the quantity and whether you order warmers and coolers together, so we do not list figures on the site. Tell us your number of rooms and the units you have in mind, and we will send a current price on WhatsApp or by phone.
